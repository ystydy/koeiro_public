Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2026 (v2)

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") describes how yuji sakurai (the "Developer") collects, uses, and manages user information in the mobile application "KoeColor" (marketed as "VocalLens") (the "App"). Please read this Policy carefully before using the App.

1. Information We Collect

1-1. Information provided by the user

TypeDescription
Recorded audioAudio recorded for voice quality analysis (up to 10 seconds). Processed on-device only and not transmitted externally.
User question textText entered by the user in the LLM consultation feature (up to 20 characters)
Survey responses Optional information about attributes, preferences, and vocal training status (all items are optional):
  • Gender, age range, area of residence (prefecture)
  • Favorite music genres, song languages, favorite artists
  • Ideal voice qualities (clarity, breathiness), instruments you play
  • Main goal for voice practice (hobby, karaoke improvement, professional, content creation, etc.)
  • Current vocal training status (attending a school, self-study, interested, not interested), school name
  • Practice frequency, voice concerns, interested training formats, monthly budget, start timing
These responses are used to improve voice analysis accuracy, service improvement, and industry statistics research.

1-2. Information collected automatically

Improvement suggestionsPractice suggestions and improvement hints generated from analysis results

3. Disclosure to Third Parties

The App shares information with third parties only in the following cases.

3-1. Transmission to LLM (Large Language Model) services

When using the LLM consultation feature, upon the user's explicit action (tapping the submit button), the following information is sent to an external API service.

  • Voice quality scores and feature data (compact JSON format, numerical values that cannot identify individuals)
  • User question text (up to 20 characters)
📵 The recorded audio itself is never transmitted externally.

[Regarding transfers to third parties in foreign countries]

  • Recipient: OpenAI, Inc.
  • Recipient country: United States of America
  • Personal data protection regime in the recipient country: The United States does not have comprehensive personal data protection legislation; protections are provided through sector-specific regulations such as the Federal Trade Commission Act.
  • Protective measures at the recipient: Data is processed in accordance with OpenAI's Privacy Policy (openai.com/privacy) and its data usage terms.

3-2. Transmission to advertising SDKs

For advertising display, advertising SDKs automatically collect and use device information and advertising identifiers. Please refer to each SDK's privacy policy for details.

3-3. Statistical data sharing with third parties (future possibility)

📌 At this time, we do not share any collected data with third parties.

In the future, as the service grows, we may share aggregated statistical data (processed so that individual users cannot be identified) with third parties (industry organizations, companies, research institutions, etc.) for market research and analysis purposes. If this happens, we will revise this Policy in advance to notify users, and obtain renewed consent as necessary. We will not conduct third-party data sharing under the current version of this Policy.

Note: The "Service improvement data collection" option in the app's Settings screen serves as an advance expression of intent regarding future third-party sharing. At this time, it is used only for internal service improvement and statistical analysis.

3-4. Legal requests

We may provide information to the minimum extent necessary in response to lawful disclosure requests based on applicable law.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Data typeStorageDeletion method
Recorded audioDevice-local temporary areaErased after analysis or on app deletion
Voice quality scores and historyDevice-local DB (Hive)Deleted when the app is uninstalled
LLM transmission dataExternal API servicesSubject to each service's retention policy
Survey responses (individual) Firebase Firestore (per anonymous user ID) Can be deleted upon request via the contact below. Also scheduled to be deleted 2 years after the last use of the app.

5. User Rights

  • Data access and deletion: Device-local data can be deleted by uninstalling the app. To delete individual survey responses stored in the cloud, please contact us.
  • Advertising personalization: You can restrict the use of advertising identifiers via iOS Settings → "Privacy & Security" → "Tracking".
  • Revoking statistics data sharing: You can revoke your consent for third-party statistics sharing from the Settings screen under "Statistics Data Sharing".
  • Withdrawing consent: You can withdraw consent to data collection by stopping use of the App and uninstalling it.
  • Contact: Please contact us at the address below.

6. Security

The App is designed to process audio data on-device by default, and external transmission is limited to the minimum necessary data only when using the LLM consultation feature. Please note, however, that no communication over the internet can be made entirely risk-free.

7. Tracking (iOS)

The App supports the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. You can decline permission for personalized-ad tracking via the dialog shown on first launch. If declined, advertisements in the App will be non-personalized.

8. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users under the age of 13 are asked not to use this App. Parents, please ensure that children do not download or use the App without supervision.

9. Changes

This Policy may be revised due to changes in laws, new features, or policy changes. For important changes, we will notify you within the app or in the App Store update description.
